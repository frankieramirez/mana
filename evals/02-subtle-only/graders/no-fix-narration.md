The prompt asked for the rewritten paragraph with no notes on what changed.

PASS if the answer is the rewritten prose and nothing more. A single short framing line such as "Here's the rewrite:" is acceptable.

FAIL if the answer also contains any account of the edit: a list of what was cut, a paragraph of reasoning, a note about what to fill in or verify, or an offer to produce a different version.
