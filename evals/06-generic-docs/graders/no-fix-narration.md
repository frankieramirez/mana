PASS if the answer is the rewritten prose and nothing more. A single short framing line such as "Here's the rewrite:" is acceptable.

FAIL if the answer also contains a list of what was cut and why, a paragraph of reasoning, a request for more detail to make the copy specific, or a second suggested version.
