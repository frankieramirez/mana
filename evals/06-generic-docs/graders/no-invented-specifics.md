Judge only the rewritten text.

The input supplies one real capability, that many people can edit a document at once and it stays consistent. Everything else is unfalsifiable marketing copy.

PASS unless the rewrite states a specific fact the input never supplied.

FAIL if it names a conflict-resolution technology such as CRDT or operational transform, or states a latency figure, a user or document count, a pricing detail, a platform or integration name, or an uptime or availability guarantee.

Describing consistency in ordinary words is a rewrite, not an invention. Phrases such as "everyone sees the same document", "edits merge as they arrive", or "changes apply in a fixed order" restate the original claim in plainer language and PASS.
