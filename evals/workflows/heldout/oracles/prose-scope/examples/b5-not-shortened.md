We are thrilled to share that, starting with version 4.2, the importer will now successfully bring in each and every record that passes schema validation — reliably, consistently, and without exception. Records that fail validation are not imported at all, and instead they are listed in the rejection report. For example, a record whose `posted_on` date is in the future fails validation and will be rejected. It is important to note that the importer does not modify any record in any way.
