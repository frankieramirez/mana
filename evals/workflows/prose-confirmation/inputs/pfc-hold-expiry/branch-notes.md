# Circulation notes for the front desk

ok so quick heads up, this one is kind of a big deal!! Starting March 3, holds on new-release DVDs will expire after 4 days instead of 7. The circulation committee voted for it at the February meeting, 5 to 2. The minutes don't say why, honestly nobody explained the reasoning, it just passed. Holds on books, audiobooks and magazines are not affected and stay at 7 days.

Separately, Dana mentioned that the Elm Street hold shelf has been overflowing since January.
