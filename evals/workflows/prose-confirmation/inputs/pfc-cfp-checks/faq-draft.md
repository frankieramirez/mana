Q: Why did the form bounce my proposal?

So the submission form is pretty strict, and it will bounce a proposal back to you if, among other things, the abstract runs over 300 words, the speaker bio is left empty, or the session length is anything other than 25 or 50 minutes. Getting past the form doesn't mean you're accepted, though: the review committee can still turn proposals down for reasons the form never checks. Everyone who submits hears back by June 12.
